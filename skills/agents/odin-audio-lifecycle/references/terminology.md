# Terminology & Command Surface

## Preferred Term
Use **"listening"** (not "recording") when referring to sidecar audio capture in user-facing text, commands, and documentation.

## Supported Commands (listening only)

**Start:**
- `start listening`
- `begin listening`
- `start audio`
- `begin audio`

**Stop:**
- `stop listening`
- `end listening`
- `stop audio`
- `end audio`

**Status:**
- `listening status`
- `audio status`
- `is listening`

Avoid mixing "recording" language in responses or command parsing.
